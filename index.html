About

I'm Abdul Hadi — a 3D artist working mainly in Blender. Product visuals, environments, hard-surface models, and the occasional experiment that exists purely because the lighting looked good at 2am.

This site is where I put that work. I publish the source files too, because that's how I learned — if a scene here saves you an afternoon of guessing at shader settings, it's done its job. Credit is appreciated, not enforced.

Using the files

  1. Open any piece marked .blend and hit download.
  2. Open in Blender 4.0 or newer — textures are packed unless noted.
  3. Use it, remix it, ship it. A credit link back is always welcome.

Files are provided as-is. Commercial use is fine unless a project says otherwise.
