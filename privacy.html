Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 August 2026

This site is a portfolio of 3D renders and Blender project files run by Abdul Hadi. This page explains what happens to data when you visit.

What I collect

Nothing directly. There is no account to create, no newsletter, no contact form, and no analytics of my own. I do not build a profile of you and I have no database of visitors. If you email me, I only have whatever you put in that email.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. To do that, Google and its partners set cookies in your browser and may use them to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other sites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visits here and elsewhere on the internet.
  • You can turn off personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.com.
  • Google's own terms are in the Google Privacy & Terms.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may also serve ads on this site using their own cookies. I have no access to or control over those cookies.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which processes standard server request data such as IP address and browser type to deliver pages and protect against abuse. Typefaces load from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers to fetch them.

Downloads

The .blend files are served as plain static files. Downloading one requires no sign-in and I receive no notification of who downloaded what.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
